SIREN HEAD
Toronto Night
You are Siren Head — five storeys of weathered flesh and screaming steel, loose in a storm-lit downtown Toronto. The army is swarming the streets to stop you. Smash through them, push toward the CN Tower, and face the thing waiting in the plaza: House Head.
