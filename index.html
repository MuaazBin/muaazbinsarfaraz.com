My operating philosophy

Analytics becomes valuable when it changes the next decision.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    Start with the consequence, owner, and cadence—not the dashboard.

  2. 02

    Build the evidence

    Use the simplest rigorous method that can survive executive scrutiny.

  3. 03

    Design the operating loop

    Connect metrics to reviews, actions, owners, and follow-through.

  4. 04

    Transfer the capability

    Build teams and systems that do not depend on a single hero.
